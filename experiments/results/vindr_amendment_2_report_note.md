# Note to the VinDr reports under Amendment 2 (data source)

Required by `experiments/vindr_amendment_2_data_source.md`. The reports
`vindr_external_validation.md` (Part A) and `vindr_grounding_amendment.md` (Part B) were
generated by the frozen scripts and are left unedited; read them with this note.

## Corrections to the Part A report's fixed header
The Part A script prints "VinDr-CXR test split ... 5-radiologist consensus labels", which
describes the original (PhysioNet) data source. Under Amendment 2 and its deviation 1 the data
are:
- 3,000 images sampled label-independently from the **training split** of the Kaggle
  VinBigData release (`vindr_kaggle_sample.json`, commit 10ccfec);
- labels: a finding is present if **at least 2 of 3 independent readers** boxed it
  (`D:/datasets/vindr-cxr-kaggle-derived/derivation_report.json`: 0 images excluded; images
  marked by exactly one reader: cardiomegaly 82, consolidation 49, pleural effusion 78,
  atelectasis 30, pneumothorax 5);
- **pulmonary edema: not labelled in this data source** (no edema class). The report's
  "0 positives / not evaluable" is not a negative result.

## Interpretation caution, to be reported with the results
Discrimination on these images is far higher than on the development data (p_present AUC
0.983-0.999 vs 0.80-0.96 on Indiana/NIH), and no positive case was assigned to REFUTING. The
pre-specified limitation applies with more force than anticipated: the images come from VinDr's
training split, and CheXagent's training collection is reported to include VinDr-CXR (to be
verified against the CheXagent publication), so CheXagent may have seen these images, their
labels and their radiologist boxes. The Part A and Part B results may therefore overstate
performance on unseen data; this applies to Part B's H1 (box laterality) as well. It weighs
less against H2, since memorised boxes would tend to resist, not follow, a wrong-side prompt.
