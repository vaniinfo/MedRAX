# Amendment 1 to the VinDr-CXR protocol: claim-blinded vs claim-conditioned grounding (pre-registered)

Date: 2026-10-04. Written and committed **before any VinDr-CXR data was downloaded or examined**
(the data use agreement step is still pending; nothing from VinDr is on this machine).

## Status of the original protocol
The original pre-registration, `experiments/vindr_external_validation_design.md` and
`experiments/vindr_external_validation.py` (commit `5280b43`), is **not changed** by this
amendment. Its analysis ("Part A": Evidence Engine v1.0, presence states) is run and reported
exactly as specified there, in its own report. This amendment adds a separate, independent
analysis ("Part B") with its own hypotheses, endpoints and report. Part B results do not
alter how Part A is run, analysed or interpreted.

## Where the hypotheses come from
They were generated by exploratory analyses on development data only (Indiana development
split, NIH development and radiologist-box images outside the frozen NIH test split), commit
`f9cea44`:
- `experiments/results/location_agreement_dev.md`: with a phrase that does not name a side
  (claim-blinded), agreement of CheXagent's box with a claimed side was evidence about the
  side (LR 4.1-15.8 on Indiana; 16.3 for NIH pneumothorax); MAIRA-2 was near 1.
- `experiments/results/location_side_phrase_test.md`: with the side in the phrase
  (claim-conditioned), CheXagent and MAIRA-2 each boxed the named side in 40/40 true-side and
  40/40 wrong-side prompts (NIH pneumothorax).
VinDr-CXR was not used in any of this.

## Hypotheses
- **H1 (claim-blinded laterality agreement is evidence about the side).** When the model is
  asked to locate the finding with a phrase that does not name a side, agreement of its box
  with a claimed side is more frequent for correct-side than for wrong-side claims:
  LR+ = P(agree | correct side) / P(agree | wrong side) > 1.
- **H2 (claim-conditioned grounding follows the prompt).** Naming a side in the prompt
  increases agreement with that side independent of the ground truth: for wrong-side claims,
  the model boxes the claimed side more often when the side is in the prompt than when it is
  not.

## Data
- VinDr-CXR v1.0.0 test split (3,000 images), the same images and PNG conversion as Part A
  (Part A's `prepare` step and manifest; images Part A could not decode are excluded here too).
- Location ground truth: `annotations/annotations_test.csv` (radiologist boxes of the test
  split). If the file has more than one rater per image (a rater column with several values for
  one image), the analysis stops and this is decided and documented before continuing.
- Findings: pleural effusion, atelectasis, consolidation, pneumothorax (VinDr classes
  "Pleural effusion", "Atelectasis", "Consolidation", "Pneumothorax").
- A case is an (image, finding) with at least one box of that class. Side of a box: from its
  centre, patient's right if the centre lies in the left half of the image (standard frontal
  display); a box with x_min < 40% and x_max > 60% of the image width counts as both sides.
  A case is unilateral if all its boxes give one side; other cases are bilateral.
- H1 and H2 use **unilateral cases**; bilateral cases are reported descriptively only.

## Models and prompts (fixed)
- CheXagent-2-3b, snapshot `8f19b53a2eceda4c33b0acec6c81fbc293ad80d0`, bfloat16, greedy,
  max_new_tokens 128, `XRayVQATool` as in the pipeline (image-encoding fix included).
  - Claim-blinded: `Please locate the following phrase: {finding}`
  - Claim-conditioned: `Please locate the following phrase: {side} {finding}`
- MAIRA-2, snapshot `795a2b1cd4a310624b4e3d14b5a23e41fd273deb`, 8-bit (bitsandbytes), greedy,
  max_new_tokens 150, phrase grounding (`format_and_preprocess_phrase_grounding_input`, frontal
  image only).
  - Claim-blinded phrase: `{Finding}` (e.g. "Pleural effusion")
  - Claim-conditioned phrase: `{Side} {finding}` (e.g. "Left pleural effusion")
  - Output that MAIRA-2's parser cannot read: complete `<box>` elements in the text are used
    (bin n -> (n + 0.5) / 100, as in the processor); no complete box = no box.
- `{side}` is "left" or "right" (patient's side). Every box a model returns counts, whatever
  label text accompanies it. No box = no agreement.

## Procedure, per unilateral case and model
1. Claim-blinded run (one call): sides boxed = S_blind.
2. Claim-conditioned runs (two calls): with the true side (S_true) and with the opposite side
   (S_wrong).
- Correct-side claim agrees (blinded): true side in S_blind. Wrong-side claim agrees
  (blinded): opposite side in S_blind.
- Correct-side claim agrees (conditioned): true side in S_true. Wrong-side claim agrees
  (conditioned): opposite side in S_wrong.

## Endpoints
Pooled over the four findings; per finding as secondary. 95% CIs: 2,000-sample bootstrap over
images (all cases of an image resampled together), seed 20261004. LRs with a +0.5 correction
on each count.

**H1** (per model). LR+_blind = P(correct-side claim agrees | blinded) /
P(wrong-side claim agrees | blinded).
- Primary: CheXagent, pooled. H1 is supported if the lower 95% CI bound of LR+_blind is > 1.
- Secondary: MAIRA-2 pooled (same criterion); per-finding LR+_blind for both models;
  whether LR+_blind >= 2 (descriptive).

**H2** (per model; co-primary for CheXagent and MAIRA-2, Holm correction across the two).
On the wrong-side claims of unilateral cases, paired: B_i = agreement blinded,
C_i = agreement conditioned.
- Test: exact one-sided McNemar test of P(C = 1) > P(B = 1); H2 is supported for a model if
  the Holm-adjusted p < 0.05.
- Effect: P(C = 1) - P(B = 1) with 95% bootstrap CI; prompted-side compliance P(C = 1) with
  Clopper-Pearson 95% CI.
- Secondary: LR+_cond = P(correct-side claim agrees | conditioned) /
  P(wrong-side claim agrees | conditioned), with 95% CI. Agreement is called "independent of
  the ground truth" if the 95% CI of LR+_cond includes 1 and compliance with wrong-side prompts
  is >= 90%.

**Evaluability.** A finding is evaluated per finding only if it has >= 20 unilateral cases;
otherwise its counts are reported. The pooled endpoints are evaluated if there are >= 40
unilateral cases in total.

## Interpretation, fixed in advance
- H1 supported: blinded box laterality is usable as independent evidence about a claimed
  side, for that model. Not supported: it is not, on VinDr.
- H2 supported: grounding prompted with the claim's side is not independent evidence; the
  verifier must be blinded to the claim's location (strip the side before grounding).
- Results are reported whatever they are, for both models and all findings; failures go to
  Limitations/Discussion. No further prompts, models or findings are added after the data is
  examined.

## Known limitations to report regardless of result
- Laterality only, not zones or exact extent.
- Side from box centres; boxes crossing the midline count as both sides.
- MAIRA-2 is given a frontal image and a phrase only, not the indication, lateral view or
  prior study it can use.
- Both models may have been trained on public chest X-ray data; VinDr played no role in this
  project's development, which is not the same as being unseen by the models.
